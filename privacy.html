Privacy Policy for SmsGuard

Last Updated: August 2026

SmsGuard respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, use, store, and process when you use SmsGuard.

SmsGuard is a messaging and spam protection application. The app uses certain permissions only to provide messaging, filtering, blocking, contact display, dual SIM, notification, premium, and security features.

Important: SmsGuard uses SMS/MMS message content, sender and recipient phone numbers, and contact information only for the app’s messaging and protection features. SmsGuard does not use this information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information SmsGuard May Access and Use

1. SMS and MMS Messages

SmsGuard may access SMS and MMS messages on your device to provide its core messaging features.

This includes:

  • Sending SMS messages
  • Receiving SMS messages
  • Receiving MMS messages
  • Displaying SMS/MMS conversations
  • Managing message history
  • Blocking unwanted messages
  • Filtering spam messages
  • Applying keyword filters
  • Supporting scheduled messages and message-related app features

SMS and MMS message content is processed to provide messaging and protection features inside the app. Spam detection, number filtering, keyword filtering, and message management are processed locally on your device.

SmsGuard does not upload SMS/MMS message content to SmsGuard or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting or spam analysis.

SmsGuard does not use SMS/MMS message content for advertising or marketing purposes.

2. Phone Numbers

SmsGuard may access and use sender and recipient phone numbers found in SMS/MMS conversations on your device.

This includes phone numbers found in regular, blocked, spam, sent, and received conversations.

Phone number information is used for:

  • Sending and receiving SMS messages
  • Showing conversation lists
  • Displaying senders and recipients
  • Recognizing phone numbers
  • Blocking selected phone numbers
  • Applying number-based filters
  • Applying spam protection rules
  • Supporting dual SIM messaging
  • Matching phone numbers with saved contacts when contact permission is granted
  • Displaying saved contact names in message lists, conversations, and notifications

Phone number information is used only for SmsGuard’s messaging and protection features.

SmsGuard does not upload sender or recipient phone numbers to SmsGuard or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting or spam analysis.

SmsGuard does not use phone numbers for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Contacts

If you grant contact permission, SmsGuard may access contacts stored on your device.

Contacts are used to match phone numbers with saved contact names and display those names in conversations, message lists, recipient selection screens, and notifications.

SmsGuard does not upload your contact list to SmsGuard or Firebase backend services for spam analysis, advertising, or marketing.

Contact information is used only for contact matching and messaging-related features.

4. Dual SIM and Phone State Information

On supported devices, SmsGuard may access phone state or SIM subscription information to provide dual SIM messaging features.

This may be used to:

  • Show which SIM card is used for messages
  • Show which SIM received a message
  • Allow SIM selection when sending messages
  • Improve messaging behavior on dual SIM devices

This information is used only for messaging-related features.

5. Spam Protection

SmsGuard provides spam protection using features such as:

  • Number-based filters
  • Keyword filters
  • Unknown sender blocking
  • Manual spam-folder actions
  • Local spam detection

These spam protection features are processed locally on the user’s device.

SmsGuard does not operate a community spam-reporting system and does not upload sender phone numbers or SMS/MMS message content to SmsGuard or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting or spam analysis.

6. Device Identifiers and Technical Information

SmsGuard uses third-party services including Google services such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Integrity

Depending on the service and device configuration, these services may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, analytics information, purchase-related information, authentication information, fraud-prevention information, or security signals according to their own privacy policies.

The free version of SmsGuard uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

SmsGuard does not provide SMS/MMS message content, sender or recipient phone numbers, or your contact list to Google AdMob for advertising targeting.

Google’s Privacy Policy is available here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Information Is Used

SmsGuard uses information to provide and maintain app features, including:

  • Sending and receiving SMS/MMS messages
  • Displaying conversations
  • Showing sender and recipient phone numbers
  • Recognizing phone numbers in conversations
  • Blocking unwanted numbers
  • Filtering unwanted messages
  • Applying number and keyword filters
  • Showing saved contact names
  • Supporting dual SIM messaging
  • Sending message notifications
  • Supporting scheduled messages
  • Providing Premium features and purchase restoration
  • Displaying advertisements in the free version
  • Performing security and abuse-prevention checks

Data Storage

SmsGuard may store app data locally on your device to make the app work properly.

This may include:

  • Message history
  • Conversation information
  • Sender and recipient phone numbers
  • Blocked numbers
  • Trusted numbers
  • Keyword filters
  • Spam settings
  • Scheduled messages
  • App preferences
  • Premium and reward-related settings

SmsGuard does not operate backend servers that store users’ personal SMS/MMS conversations.

Data Sharing

SmsGuard does not sell your personal messaging data.

SmsGuard does not use SMS/MMS message content, sender or recipient phone numbers, or contact information for advertising or marketing purposes.

SmsGuard does not upload SMS/MMS message content, sender or recipient phone numbers, or your contact list to SmsGuard or Firebase backend services for community spam reporting or spam analysis.

Third-party services used by the app, including Google AdMob, Firebase, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Integrity, may process technical, advertising, analytics, purchase, authentication, diagnostic, or security information as required to provide their respective services and according to their own privacy policies.

Permissions Used by SmsGuard

SmsGuard may request the following permissions depending on your device and app usage:

  • SMS permissions for sending, receiving, reading, displaying, filtering, and managing SMS/MMS messages
  • Contacts permission for matching phone numbers with saved contact names
  • Phone state permission for SIM and dual SIM messaging features
  • Notification permission for message notifications
  • Camera or media-related access when the user chooses to attach media
  • Internet permission for advertisements, billing, analytics, authentication, security checks, and other online app services

Permissions are used only for the features described in this Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored app data at any time by:

  • Deleting messages or blocked items inside the app where available
  • Clearing SmsGuard app data from Android system settings
  • Uninstalling the application

Because message history, filters, spam settings, and most messaging-related information are stored locally on your device, clearing app data or uninstalling SmsGuard removes this locally stored application data.

Children’s Privacy

SmsGuard is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. SmsGuard does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

SmsGuard is designed to process sensitive messaging information locally whenever possible.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect application data and reduce unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
