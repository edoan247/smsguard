Privacy Policy for SmsGuard

Last Updated: June 2026

SmsGuard respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, collect, use, store, and process when you use SmsGuard.

SmsGuard is a messaging and spam protection application. The app uses certain permissions only to provide messaging, filtering, blocking, contact display, dual SIM, and protection features.

Important: SmsGuard uses SMS/MMS message content, phone numbers, and contact information only for the app’s messaging and protection features. SmsGuard does not use this information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information SmsGuard May Access, Collect, and Use

1. SMS and MMS Messages

SmsGuard may access SMS and MMS messages on your device to provide its core messaging features.

This includes:

  • Sending SMS messages
  • Receiving SMS messages
  • Receiving MMS messages
  • Displaying SMS/MMS conversations
  • Managing message history
  • Blocking unwanted messages
  • Filtering spam messages
  • Applying keyword filters
  • Supporting scheduled messages and message-related app features

SMS and MMS message content is processed to provide messaging and protection features inside the app. SmsGuard does not use SMS/MMS message content for advertising or marketing purposes.

2. Phone Numbers

SmsGuard may access, collect, and use sender and recipient phone numbers found in SMS/MMS conversations on your device. This is not limited only to spam or blocked messages. It includes phone numbers found in SMS/MMS conversations inside the app.

Phone number information is used for:

  • Sending and receiving SMS messages
  • Showing conversation lists
  • Displaying senders and recipients in conversations
  • Recognizing phone numbers
  • Blocking selected phone numbers
  • Applying number-based spam filters
  • Applying keyword filters to conversations
  • Showing dual SIM information
  • Matching phone numbers with saved contacts when contact permission is granted
  • Displaying saved contact names in message lists, conversations, and notifications

Phone number information is used only for SmsGuard’s messaging and protection features. SmsGuard does not use phone numbers for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Contacts

If you grant contact permission, SmsGuard may access contacts stored on your device.

Contacts are used to show saved contact names instead of only phone numbers in conversations, message lists, and notifications.

Contact information is used only for contact matching and messaging-related features. SmsGuard does not use contact information for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Dual SIM and Phone State Information

On supported devices, SmsGuard may access phone state or SIM subscription information to provide dual SIM messaging features.

This may be used to:

  • Show which SIM card is used for messages
  • Allow SIM selection when sending messages
  • Improve the messaging experience on dual SIM devices

This information is used only for messaging-related features.

5. Device Identifiers and Technical Information

SmsGuard may use third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, Firebase, and Google Play Integrity services.

These services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, purchase-related information, security signals, or technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Google’s Privacy Policy is available here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Spam Reports

SmsGuard may allow users to report suspicious or spam messages to help improve spam protection.

Spam reports do not include your actual phone number, SMS/MMS message content, or contact list. SmsGuard may use limited technical or anonymized information to improve spam filtering and app security.

How Information Is Used

SmsGuard uses information only to provide and improve app features, including:

  • Sending and receiving SMS/MMS messages
  • Displaying conversations
  • Showing sender and recipient phone numbers
  • Recognizing phone numbers in conversations
  • Blocking unwanted numbers
  • Filtering spam messages
  • Applying keyword filters
  • Showing saved contact names
  • Supporting dual SIM messaging
  • Providing premium features and purchase restoration
  • Displaying ads in the free version
  • Improving app security and preventing abuse

Data Storage

SmsGuard may store app data locally on your device to make the app work properly.

This may include:

  • Message history
  • Conversation information
  • Sender and recipient phone numbers
  • Blocked numbers
  • Keyword filters
  • Spam filter settings
  • Scheduled messages
  • App preferences
  • Premium and reward-related settings

SmsGuard does not operate servers that store users’ personal SMS/MMS conversations.

Data Sharing

SmsGuard does not use your SMS/MMS message content, phone numbers, or contact information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Some third-party services used by the app, such as Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, Firebase, or Google Play Integrity, may process limited technical information, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, purchase-related information, or security signals as required to provide their services.

Permissions Used by SmsGuard

SmsGuard may request the following permissions depending on your device and app usage:

  • SMS permissions for sending, receiving, reading, and managing SMS/MMS messages
  • Contacts permission for displaying saved contact names
  • Phone state permission for dual SIM and messaging-related features
  • Notification permission for message notifications
  • Camera or media permissions when the user chooses to attach media to messages
  • Internet permission for ads, billing, security checks, and app services

Permissions are used only for the features described in this Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

You can delete app data at any time by:

  • Deleting messages or blocked items inside the app where available
  • Clearing SmsGuard app data from Android system settings
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Since most message and filter data is stored locally on your device, clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data from your device.

Children’s Privacy

SmsGuard is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

SmsGuard is designed to process personal messaging data locally whenever possible. We use reasonable measures to protect app data and reduce unauthorized access.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
